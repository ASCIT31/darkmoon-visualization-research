# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve this research record.

## What is welcome

- Corrections of facts, broken links or references (please cite a primary source).
- Replications: if you measure something listed as « not measured » in [benchmarks/](benchmarks/README.md) (for example a usability test of the « Evidence-Backed / Possible » vocabulary), open an issue with your protocol and data.
- New references with a verifiable DOI or URL.

## Rules

1. **Evidence labels.** Every new statement carries a label: VERIFIED, OBSERVED, SIMULATED, HYPOTHESIS, SUPERSEDED or UNKNOWN (see [README › Conventions](README.md#conventions)).
2. **No private data.** No client names, real IP addresses, hostnames, e-mail addresses, credentials, tokens or private repository links. Screenshots must use synthetic or public lab data.
3. **Screenshots.** PNG only, compressed (palette PNG or `pngquant`/`optipng`), named `product-feature-theme-viewport-version.png`, with an entry in the folder's `metadata.json` (product, repository, commit, version, date, data source, viewport, theme, description, status `real` | `mock-up`). Label mock-ups as `mock-up`.
4. **No third-party assets** (fonts, icon packs, UI-kit images) unless their licence allows redistribution; keep the repository under 60 MB.
5. **Rejected attempts are results.** Do not delete a failure; mark it SUPERSEDED and explain.
6. English, plain wording, one paragraph per line in Markdown.

## Process

Open an issue first for anything larger than a typo, then a pull request against `main`. By contributing you agree that your contribution is licensed under the GNU AGPL-3.0 ([LICENSE](LICENSE)).
